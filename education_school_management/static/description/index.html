School Management System — Complete Institution Control

The School Management module provides a centralized
digital platform to manage day-to-day academic and
administrative operations.It streamlines student lifecycle
management from admission to graduation within a single
integrated system.Attendance tracking is modernized through
QR-based scanning for fast and accurate daily monitoring.
Administrators can manage teachers, classes, subjects, exams,
and timetables from one dashboard.The system simplifies staff
management by storing roles, contact details, and activity
status securely.Financial and administrative records such as bank
details and institutional settings are organized in one place.
